\section{Conclusion}
What a model says it used and what it used are different things, and for audio and video only the first has been cheap to obtain.
OmniTrace narrows this gap. Reading the attention of the generation pass as mass over source units gives, at no extra cost, attributions that hold up under intervention in three modalities and three models, and that come close to perturbation methods where the fixed setting fits the model.
The causal protocol is independent of the method: with content-free placeholders and size-matched controls, any attribution method can be tested on continuous inputs.
The same tests show where an untrained reading is not enough (fine localization in video, sources of very different sizes, a model whose evidence lies outside the late layers) and make these gaps measurable.
