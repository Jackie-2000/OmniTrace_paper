\section{Conclusion}
What a model says it used and what it used are different things, and for audio and video only the first has been cheap to obtain.
OmniTrace narrows this gap. Reading the attention of the generation pass as mass over source units gives, at no extra cost and with one setting, attributions that hold up under intervention in three modalities on three models, and that come close to perturbation methods on the model the setting was chosen on; where it falls short, training the head weights closes most of the gap.
The causal protocol is independent of the method: with content-free placeholders and size-matched controls, any attribution method can be tested on continuous inputs.
